Ant Arena

A 1v1 or 2v2 ant colony battle that runs in the browser. Gather food, hatch workers, soldiers, spitters and other castes, evolve your colony, and wreck the enemy nests.

Play with a friend

  1. The host opens Host a game, picks 1v1 or 2v2 and sends the join code or link. Keep that tab open and in front while you play.
  2. Friends paste the code (or the whole link) above and press Join.

Join codes go through free public relays, so nobody needs to open a port. A few strict networks still can't connect; if Join times out, try the host's own link.
