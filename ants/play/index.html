Evolutions

🦷 Mandibles: army damage
🛡️ Chitin: army HP, tougher nest
🧪 Acid glands: spitter range, splash
🦵 Long legs: every ant faster
🌾 Big crops: workers carry more
🍯 Royal jelly: nurses heal faster and tend workers
Shift+key
Cancel it (or right-click its button); full refund

Tier III (your one mastery) also changes a rule: Mandibles cleave a second enemy, Chitin regrows nest and buildings, Acid glands leave slowing puddles, Long legs ignore rain and flood, Big crops carry 2 pellets and pay +10%, Royal jelly heals a second patient. Hover the button for the details. Gold chevrons over an ant mark a veteran (3 kills: +10% damage and HP) or elite (8 kills: +20%).

Research costs food (plus pellets from tier II), runs one at a time and takes seconds. Only one evolution can reach tier III. Icons on the top bar show every colony's evolutions.

🐜 Ant Arena

Isometric colony battle · 1v1 or 2v2

Bug-tech warfare. Please do not feed the queen after midnight.
How to play
  1. Pick a room code and choose 1v1 or 2v2; send friends the link (or fill with bots).
  2. Z workers gather food. X soldiers fight, C spitters beat soldier blobs, V majors (a pellet) beat spitters and smash nests. N nurses heal, B hawkers fly in to raid workers.
  3. T Y U I O evolve your colony (only one evolution reaches tier III). G J K L abilities: trail, alarm, acid volley, queen's call. M builds caches, acid mounds, outposts.
  4. Fog of war: you see around your ants and buildings. Every 25 s a chaos die rolls: in an earthquake, ants beside a nest or finished building are safe.
  5. Hold the 🌿 aphid farms for +1 food/s; kill a corner 🕷 spider for loot.
  6. Destroy every enemy nest (a falling nest moves into its outpost). At 12:00 more farms wins; tied: sudden death. ? lists every key.
