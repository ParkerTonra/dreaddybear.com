Ant Arena asset credits
=======================

Sound effects (public/assets/sfx/)
  kenney-impact/  Kenney "Impact Sounds" 1.0, https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds, CC0 (License.txt alongside)
                  Used: impactPunch_medium x5, impactSoft_medium x5, impactPlate_heavy x3, impactSoft_heavy x3,
                  impactGlass_light x3, impactMining x3, impactPlank_medium x3, impactBell_heavy x2, footstep_grass x3
  kenney-scifi/   Kenney "Sci-Fi Sounds", https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds, CC0 (License.txt alongside)
                  Used: slime_000, forceField x2, laserRetro x3, lowFrequency_explosion x2
Files are the unmodified originals (already .ogg). Both packs are CC0: no credit is required; it is given anyway
(Sound effects line in the ? help screen).
Not used: the Monster Sound Pack (OGA, WAV only; needs conversion, see HANDOFF ant18/audio).
ants.ogg: the game's own song (Parker, ants v3), in the lobby and in matches.
dusk_colony.ogg, ember_dusk.ogg, swarm.ogg: original placeholder tracks composed with Fontbard (Old School RuneScape soundfont), shuffled after ants.ogg.

3D model (rendered to the worker ant sprite sheet, assets-src/worker/; tools/blender/render-worker.py)
  "Ant 3D Model + Rigging + Animated (Low-poly-ish)" by mujtaba-io, https://opengameart.org/content/ant-3d-model-rigging-animated-low-poly-ish, CC0 (no credit required; given anyway)
  Rendered with Blender 4.2 LTS (a portable download, not committed). The model's own black material was replaced by a neutral one.

3D models (rendered to the building sprite sheet, public/assets/buildings/; ants/tools/blender/render-building.py)
  Quaternius "Nature Crops Pack" (Jan 2020: Mushroom_1..4, Wheat_Crop) and "Ultimate Nature Pack" (Jun 2019: TreeStump, WoodLog, Rock_1, Rock_Moss_1..7),
  https://quaternius.com, CC0 (no credit required; given anyway). Rendered with Blender 4.2 LTS; materials recoloured, composed into small dioramas.
  ant63 nest and outpost: the same Ultimate Nature Pack (Rock_2, Rock_4, Rock_6, Rock_Moss_2, TreeStump_Moss, Grass, Grass_2, Grass_Short); the earthen
  anthill, entrance hole, crumbs and flag are plain Blender primitives. Both are cells of the same buildings.png (the nest is the big cell on the right).
